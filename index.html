About Me

Design and code, in one head.

I started in visual design and keep going until I could ship exactly what I draw. That means zero friction in hand-offs, fewer surprises, and web apps that match the mockup to the exact pixel.

Most projects suffer from the "handoff gap"—designers draw interfaces that engineers struggle to build, or developers write code that compromises visual elegance. My mission is to bridge this gap completely. By thinking in component structures while designing in Figma, and respecting typographic rhythm and motion while coding in CSS and JavaScript, I create seamless digital products that look stunning and perform reliably.

Currently, I partner with forward-thinking teams, startups, and agencies worldwide to turn raw concepts into tested user flows, scalable design systems, and responsive production code.
